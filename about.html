WHO WE ARE

A Dental Clinic Built on Trust, Experience & Care

Jansewa Dental Clinic has been serving patients since 1995, building its journey around a simple commitment — to provide dependable dental care with compassion and integrity.

Since 2009, Dr. Reeshu Bedi has been operating the clinic and continuing this commitment through experienced, patient-focused dental care.

Today, Jansewa Dental Clinic brings together experience, modern dental practices and a personalised approach to help patients maintain healthy teeth, gums and confident smiles.
